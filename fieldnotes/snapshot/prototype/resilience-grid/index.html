03 · The business model

Utility, insurer, and community fund Firebreak.

Utility

Supports prevention and coordination.

Less ignition exposure. Its infrastructure protected.

Insurer

Funds or invests in regional risk mitigation.

Mitigation that supports underwriting.

Community · local government

Supports protection for the area.

The area stays insurable.
