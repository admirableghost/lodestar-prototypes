01 / The network

The pieces exist.The agreementis the proposition.

Forty-three companies across twelve countries build these layers, and utilities, fire agencies and insurers already buy from them. What does not arise on its own is a durable operating and economic arrangement between parties whose incentives are currently fragmented.

What is actually missing

These layers already meet at incidents. What is missing is the standing arrangement between them.

Who commissions the work, who verifies it independently, who pays for which part, and what evidence would change an underwriting decision. That institutional layer is what Resilience Grid is testing — not the hardware.

Detection is notthe product.Coordination is.

A fault on the Mill Creek lateral at 14:31, wind already over the ridge. An alert would have ended in somebody's inbox.

Who is allowed to do what

Each party's agent acts only inside permissions agreed in advance, and every action it takes is written to one auditable record. Bounded actions — de-energising a lateral, rolling an engine — are pre-authorised and narrow. Consequential calls are not delegated.

The operating agreement · hypothetical

  1. 1A regional consortium commissions prevention services for the interface — fuel treatment, sensing, response capacity.
  2. 2Parties fund the components whose avoided costs they already carry. The utility funds ignition prevention; public agencies fund evacuation capacity and fuel treatment on public land.
  3. 3Providers deliver under defined operating protocols — what may be actioned automatically, what requires a human, and who holds command.
  4. 4An independent evaluator measures interventions performed, operational outcomes, relevant exposure change, and the uncertainty and failure cases alongside them.
  5. 5If agreed evidence thresholds are met, participating carriers commit in advance to reconsider specified capacity and underwriting decisions — geography reopened for consideration, eligibility reviewed, limits and pricing inputs revisited.

Clause 5 is the commitment that makes the rest bankable, and it is a commitment to review, not to cover. No automatic coverage follows from participation.

The Aspen Fire Protection District buys from Seneca, Pano AI and OroraTech, with the Seneca and OroraTech purchases funded by foundation and donor money — a district already not paying alone. 2026. We have not established a scaled regional example of the complete operating loop above.

04 / The loop

The drone isthe easy part.Showing it workedis the business.

The operating loop

01 Observe

Three independent sources, one ignition. Cheap, and getting cheaper.

02 Intervene

De‑energise, cut fuel, put water on the head of it. Purchasable today.

03 Verify

Independently record what work happened: which parcel, to what standard, on what date.

04 Evaluate

Estimate risk reduction against a credible comparison, and carry the uncertainty with it.

05 Learn & update

Revise which interventions are worth repeating, and what they imply for risk and coverage.

Four separate questions, and they are not the same question: did the intervention happen as intended; did it change exposure; how much did expected risk move; and does that justify an underwriting decision. An inconclusive result, a failed intervention and no change in terms are all valid outcomes of this loop — and the commonest ones early on.

Where an edge might sit · hypothesis

The sensors and the aircraft are for sale to everyone. We found no public evidence of insurer‑exclusive data rights in the deals we reviewed, so the candidate is not the data itself.

The claim
Becoming the carrier that translates independently verified prevention outcomes into underwriting capacity and terms
What compounds
Operating history connecting intervention to outcome to underwriting — if it is long enough to beat buying the same feed
Also possible
Being the party that convenes the payers. A position, not a technology
Not an edge
Commodity sensing, vendor data, priority access to aircraft, the partner list

Every line above is a hypothesis. We have not established a scaled regional example of this complete operating loop, and a mandate like Colorado's could make the translation step table stakes rather than an advantage.

When prevention cannot show an effect

Baloise funded hail cloud‑seeding flights with ETH Zurich from 2018 to 2023, then discontinued the programme without conclusive evidence of benefit. Announced October 2024.

The lesson is not that the intervention worked and the measurement let it down. It is that prevention which cannot demonstrate an effect does not create durable insurance value.

The question to answer first

Does the network add enough protection beyond hardening and fuel management to justify its cost?

Proven hardening and fuel management are the baseline. Detection, coordination and rapid response are the second layer, and the concept only earns its place where the incremental reduction can be shown. We do not know that it can.

05 / Market creation

The product is notthe drone. It is a marketthat might stay open.

Without the network the chain runs one way, and the household is standing at the end of it. Prevention does not create availability on its own: the chain only breaks if the intervention changes risk, the change can be credibly estimated, and capital accepts the remaining tail.

Without the network

With the network · each step conditional

Each step on the right is a condition, not a consequence: if intervention materially changes risk, and that change can be credibly measured, and capital providers accept the resulting tail, then coverage may become economically viable.
