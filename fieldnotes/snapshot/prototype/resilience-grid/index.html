02 / The moment

Detection is notthe product.Coordination is.

The event, 14:31 to 14:47. Stage one, signals corroborated: a line sensor on the Mill Creek lateral flags a fault at 14:31; the Alder Peak ridge camera sees smoke at the same span at 14:33; a thermal satellite pass confirms heat at the same coordinates at 14:34. Stage two, bounded response, pre-authorised and narrow: the utility de-energises the lateral at 14:36; a spread model runs the canyon corridor at 14:38 — a forecast, not a decision; Fire District 3 sends two engines to the canyon mouth and holds the one road out open at 14:41; four aircraft launch from the Mill Creek pad at 14:43 and hold. Stage three: the evacuation of 320 homes and the suppression drop over occupied parcels wait on the duty chief's approval. The network cannot make either call. Agents execute bounded, pre-authorised actions; humans make the consequential decisions; every action joins the evidence record. Replay plays the eight events in order; Show all marks all three stages at once.
