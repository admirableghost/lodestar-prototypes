02 / The proposition

What if they couldbuy a safer region?

The county with the service's parts marked as physical things: a maintained vegetation corridor on the slope below the ridge, a contracted ridge camera, contracted aircraft on the Mill Creek pad, contracted crews at the fire station. Resilience Grid is a regional prevention company backed by an investment from Kestrel Mutual; it maintains vegetation corridors, contracts detection and response capacity, and verifies performance and shares it with participating insurers. It contracts existing providers and coordinates their work. The community commissions the service.

03 / The moment

Before the firereaches the homes.

The canyon above Mill Creek, close. Three beats. Detect: an ignition at the canyon head and its likely path toward the 320 homes. Coordinate: Resilience Grid coordinates the utility, which de-energises its line, and the contracted aircraft, which launch and hold; Fire District 3 approves the drop and the road closure, the interventions that need its authority. Act: crews work the prepared corridor above the homes while the evacuation route stays open. The fire is contained at the corridor; no homes are reached.

06 / The venture

Three customerskeep prevention running.

The county with Mill Creek protected and the corridor maintained. Three recurring payment lines run to Resilience Grid: the municipality pays for regional readiness and response coordination; residents pay for local prevention and protection services; insurers pay for verified risk services supporting underwriting. Kestrel Mutual has two roles: an investor in Resilience Grid, and a participating insurer that buys its verified risk services. Investment is distinct from service revenue. Annual service contracts fund the next season's prevention work.
