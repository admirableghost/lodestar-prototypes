03 / Why anyone pays

A business that getspaid to change the risk.

Not a cost shared between beneficiaries. Four customers buying four defined services, each priced below the loss it prevents.

What each customer buys · one possible model

Utility

Verified fuel treatment along its own right of way, surveyed before and after

Public agencies

Evacuation‑corridor readiness, inspected and reported each season

Property owners

A maintained defensible perimeter, by subscription

Insurer

Independently verified records of what was done and what changed

The insurer is one customer of four, not the funder of the other three. The business stands up if it never signs one.

The arrangement

A regional agreement defines who commissions, who acts, who verifies, who pays, and what evidence triggers an underwriting review.

The incumbent carrier need not be the anchor funder. Its value may be translating credible evidence into underwriting decisions.

What is done, and what follows, becomes the evidence record. If risk cannot be shown to change, underwriting has no reason to change.
