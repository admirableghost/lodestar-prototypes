## What was checked

Each frame was reviewed at the resampled size for street numerals, signage,
people and brand marks. None shows a person. `your-home.jpg` has a parked car
at the far left edge, outside the on-glass crop at every tested width. No
frame carries a readable house number or sign.

## Compromises, stated plainly

- The "Harbor" house is a photographer's subject in upstate New York; it is
  not a prefab product and no builder is implied. The reference brief asked
  for "a real and compelling prefab"; this frame reads as a compact modern
  home with a lit interior, which is the feeling the offer needs, and the
  caption says only that it is illustrative.
- The existing home is in New England while the invented address stays the
  reference prototype's Bloomington, IL. The address is an invented string
  and is never tied to the photograph.
- Pacific (Seattle) and Cypress (unlocated villa) are larger homes than
  Harbor, which is the order the reference prototype's prices imply.
