Internal scenario · not visible to homeowners

Why offer Home Again?

A working business-model hypothesis. Adjust the assumptions to see the scale of the opportunity. This is not an actuarial forecast or a validated claims estimate.

Scenario inputs

The $150K input is an unvalidated hypothesis, not a reported average. It bundles possible surge-price, housing-duration and coordination savings that would each need separate measurement.

Illustrative annual scale

Home Again fees$5,040,000

Combined opportunity before costs$12,540,000

Not profit. Builder-network commitments, temporary housing provision, operating costs, risk transfer, insurer obligations and capital requirements are not modeled here. Not all losses are eligible.

Lower rebuilding volatility

Prearranged designs and production capacity may reduce post-disaster price spikes and contractor search time.

Reduce time away from home

Preplanned housing and earlier coordination could reduce displacement, uncertainty and covered living expenses.

Create a distinct paid proposition

Home Again makes the recovery service tangible at purchase and may improve trust and retention; willingness to pay still needs testing.
