Prototype / 2036

The recordis the product.

By 2036 a household delegates continuously — money, deliveries, short contracts, and machines that move on their own — and every act leaves a signed trace of what was instructed and what was done. This walks one wrong instruction through that trace, past everyone who pays before an insurer does, and into the small residual left over.

What this isEverything here is a projection: the year is 2036, the household and its loss are invented, the figures are illustrative rather than measured, and no real merchant, platform, insurer or payment network is shown as a participant.

01 / Delegation at household scale

Delegation became continuous.

A household now runs a standing fleet of agents that pay, book, sign and dispatch a few hundred times a week, nearly all of it unremarkable. What changed for an underwriter is not the volume but the mandate attached to every act, which makes delegation observable for the first time.

    02 / The mandate

    Authority, written down and signed.

        05 / The size of the residual

        Most of it never reaches the policy.

        Across a projected year of a thousand reported agent errors, 939 close at a provider, a network or a platform, and 61 reach the policy. The residual is small and its severity is long-tailed, which is the only shape in which this is insurance at all.
