agent error protection

Prototype / 2036

The recordis the product.

By 2036 a household delegates continuously, and every act leaves a signed trace of what was instructed and what was done. This follows one family through one wrong instruction, past everyone who pays before an insurer does, and into the small residual left over.

What this is

Everything here is a projection. The year is 2036, the Okonjos and their loss are invented, the figures are illustrative rather than measured, and no real merchant, platform, insurer or payment network appears.

    02 / Delegation at household scale

    Delegation became continuous.

    Six agents pay, book, sign and dispatch on Ada's behalf a few hundred times a week, nearly all of it unremarkable. What changed for an underwriter is not the volume but the mandate attached to every act, which makes delegation observable for the first time.

      03 / The mandate

      Authority, written down and signed.

      Before anything is delegated, Ada and Femi sign a mandate: scope, ceilings, forbidden acts, and how far an agent may go before it has to ask. It is the half of the record insurers have never had.

      Maintenance agent · Thursday 12 March 2036 · instruction against action

        07 / The size of the residual

        Most of it never reaches the policy.

        Across a projected year of a thousand reported agent errors, 939 close at a provider, a network or a platform, and 61 reach the policy. The residual is small and its severity is long-tailed, which is the only shape in which this is insurance at all.
