08 / Before you join

No assessments. No entitlement. No exit balance.

Three things Alma was told before her first payment.

01

No assessments

  • No power to call for more money.
  • Short means less paid, never another invoice.

02

No enforceable promise

  • Published rules, independent assessment, an appeal.
  • And no contractual right to payment.

03

Nothing to withdraw

  • Thirty days' notice, any time.
  • Contributions are spent on work, not saved.

The layer forgoes enforceability on purpose, because that is what keeps it out of the insurance code.

09 / Still unsolved

Four things ten years did not solve.

The parts a projection cannot honestly close.

Coordination is not capital, and it is not law.

Agents scheduling around a grudge solves none of the four.

  1. Willingness to pay

    • $86 a month against a credit worth perhaps a fraction of a percent.
    • Never demonstrated at scale, anywhere.

  2. The authority

    • The one invented rule.
    • In 2026: no state, no precedent.

  3. Consent

    • A geofence enforces Gerald's refusal.
    • It does not change his mind.

  4. Correlation

    • Sized for ten. A fire is not ten.
    • The answer to the common peril is prevention, not payment.

The fence line was always the wrong unit.
