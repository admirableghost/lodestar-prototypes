Concept

Prevention as market creation: a coordinated detection-to-suppression network whose product is not the hardware but a market that might stay open.

Workshop role

Fourth. The business-model centrepiece of the session, now five screens rather than six.

Screen sequence and the thought each screen must land

01 · Priced precisely, increasingly unavailable Risk can be priced to the parcel and still withdraw from the market. Precision is not availability.

02 · The network The technology already exists. What is missing is a standing agreement for who acts, who pays, and what evidence changes an insurance decision.

03 · The moment Detection is not the product. Coordination is.

04 · Paid to change the risk Four customers buying defined services. The insurer is one customer of four, not the funder of the other three. What is done, and what follows, becomes the evidence record.

05 · A market that stays open If prevention is infrastructure that keeps difficult risks insurable, what is it worth?

Key interactions

Four layer chips + Show all four Switch a layer off and the ground loses that capability. Coordination is shown by subtraction.

Replay from the start / Show all steps Runs the ignition timeline against the network.

Factual / illustrative / hypothetical

Factual

  • Pano AI and Seneca are named as real, existing capability categories. No figure, claim or partnership is attached to either.

Illustrative

  • The layer model (see / understand / act / fund) is an illustrative decomposition, not an industry taxonomy.

Hypothetical

  • Alder County, the carrier, the fund, the service prices and every figure on the glass are invented.

Every screen states once, plainly, that its content is invented. No real insurer's premium, withdrawal, book or pricing decision appears anywhere, and no invented figure is attached to a real company's name.

Product-risk assessment

CHANGED THIS ITERATION. The loop screen is removed and the deck is five screens. Its load-bearing sentence — that without credible evidence of changed risk, underwriting has no reason to change — is folded into the business-model screen as a note rather than a third body line, so no remaining screen is denser. The causal spine is intact and now runs 1–5 without a detour: unavailability, the standing agreement, live coordination, who pays, the market outcome. What is lost is the five-stage verify/evaluate diagram; the argument survives as a claim rather than a picture, which the facilitator now carries.

Known issues

  • Five screens — still the longest in the set, but no longer twice the length of everything else.
  • The evidence argument is now one line rather than a screen. It is a compression, and the facilitator carries what the diagram used to show.
