Concept

A rebuild arranged before the loss: what is committed in advance, what stays conditional, and why a bigger cheque cannot substitute for a builder.

Workshop role

Second. Moves the argument from money to recovery — what a policy could commit to while nothing is wrong.

Screen sequence and the thought each screen must land

01 · Before the loss The replacement plan, the builder and the recovery lead were all arranged while nothing was wrong. The builder reservation is stated, not counted.

02 · What is promised The commitment and the conditions sit side by side, in cards of equal weight. The conditions are not a footnote.

03 · The first days Five beats after the loss. Only the third is a claim.

Key interactions

As it stood / To current code / Code plus hardening Rewrites the fourth plan line and redraws the house — hardening adds sheathing, screened vents and a cleared edge.

Show a regional event Fills every builder slot. Exactly one commitment degrades to "Falls back"; the other three are untouched. This is the argument that money does not shorten a queue.

Replay from the start / Show all steps Drives the timeline and the ground through slab → scaffold → frame.

Factual / illustrative / hypothetical

Factual

  • No sum of money appears anywhere in the prototype — deliberately, so the argument cannot be read as a payout comparison.

Illustrative

  • Regional builder scarcity is stated qualitatively. No total capacity is shown or implied on screen 1.

Hypothetical

  • Household, region, builder, recovery lead and every quantity are invented.

Every screen states once, plainly, that its content is invented. No real insurer's premium, withdrawal, book or pricing decision appears anywhere, and no invented figure is attached to a real company's name.

Product-risk assessment

CHANGED THIS ITERATION. Screen 1’s eight-cell builder meter is gone, replaced by a qualitative reservation stamp and one line on regional scarcity. The mechanism survives — this plan is held in advance while regional capacity is short — without asserting a total nobody could know. The rebuild-standard choice, the named recovery lead and screens 2–3 are untouched.

Known issues

  • Screen 2 still uses an eight-cell meter for the regional-event condition. It was left in place deliberately: removing it would gut that screen’s interaction, and this iteration was scoped to screen 1. It is the remaining instance of countable capacity in the deck.
  • Map pins hide on a phone wherever a plate would cover them, so the mobile ground is unlabelled in some states.
  • Roughly the lower third of screens 1 and 2 at desktop is open paper — an elevation drawing is blank below its ground line.
