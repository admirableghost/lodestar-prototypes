Concept

Prevention as market creation: a coordinated detection-to-suppression network whose product is not the hardware but a market that might stay open.

Screen by screen — the thought each screen is meant to land

1. Priced precisely, increasingly unavailable Risk can be priced to the parcel and still withdraw from the market. Precision is not availability.

2. The network The technology already exists. What is missing is a standing agreement for who acts, who pays, and what evidence changes an insurance decision.

3. The moment Detection is not the product. Coordination is — the claim is not complete when the money is paid.

4. Paid to change the risk Four customers buying defined services. The insurer is one customer of four, not the funder of the other three; the business stands up if it never signs one.

5. Learning what works The drone is the easy part. Building the evidence record that says which interventions changed outcomes is the business.

6. A market that stays open If prevention is not a device or a service but infrastructure that keeps difficult risks insurable, what is it worth?

Key interactions

Four layer chips + Show all four Switch a layer off and the ground loses that capability. Coordination is shown by subtraction.

Replay from the start / Show all steps Runs the ignition timeline against the network.

What is factual and what is hypothetical

Hypothetical

  • Alder County, the carrier, the fund and every figure on the glass are invented.

Grounded in something real

  • Pano AI and Seneca are named as real existing capability categories, not as partners, and carry no invented figures.

Every screen states, once and plainly, that its content is invented. No real insurer's premium, withdrawal, book or pricing decision is stated anywhere, and no invented figure is attached to a real company's name.

Known issues

  • Six screens — twice the length of anything else in the set. It is the oldest prototype here and the most argued-through; it may now be longer than the room has patience for.
  • Screen 5 is the densest in the set and is the one most likely to need voiceover.
