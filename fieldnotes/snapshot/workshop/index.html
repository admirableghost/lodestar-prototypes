Workshop set · six prototypes

Six prototypes, one argument.

Each one is evidence for a single step. Walked in order they make the case; taken alone each is a provocation. All of them are speculative, the people and figures in them are invented, and they say so.

The customer is already a different kind of buyer → protection takes on the recovery work → a group assembles collective protection → a business gets paid to change the risk → protection enables unfamiliar activity → insurance built for AI buyers.
